# DreamTeam — Datathon FME 2026 Preparation

**Dates:** 19 October–13 November 2026 · **Effort:** 3–5 hours/person/week · **Event:** 14–15 November 2026.

**Team focus:** **tabular predictive ML** (classification and demand forecasting), based primarily on the [Schneider 2025](https://github.com/Joanfmir/DreamTeam/blob/main/reptes/2025_schneider_explicabilitat/ENUNCIAT.md) and [Mango 2025](https://github.com/Joanfmir/DreamTeam/blob/main/reptes/2025_mango_demanda/ENUNCIAT.md) challenges. See [Smadex 2025](https://github.com/Joanfmir/DreamTeam/blob/main/reptes/2025_smadex_ingressos/ENUNCIAT.md) for a secondary metric example.

## Team coordination

- [ ] **Week 1:** everyone runs a Schneider baseline; share the target, metric, and valid folds.
- [ ] **Weeks 2–3:** follow individual roadmaps, spend **at least 70%** of time practicing, and log one experiment + one takeaway weekly in [`experiments.md`](https://github.com/Joanfmir/DreamTeam/blob/main/experiments.md).
- [ ] **Week 4:** rehearse one previously unseen challenge **together**: data → baseline → validation → explanation → submission/pitch.
- [ ] **Competition day:** read all three actual briefs **before choosing**. 2025 patterns guide preparation but do **not** guarantee a matching 2026 task.
